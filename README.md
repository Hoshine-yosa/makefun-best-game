Starfall is a neon space arcade game: pilot a ship through a meteor storm, catch stars to build combos, and grab power-ups across 20 escalating waves. Keyboard and mouse controls, synthesized WebAudio sound effects and music, particles and screen shake. No build step — just serve the game folder and open it in a browser.

## Features

- **Shoot to survive**: SPACE or CLICK fires bolts — shatter meteors, down the dreadnought.
- **7 meteor types**: shards, rocks, boulders, zigzagging darts, 3-HP brutes, splitters that burst into shards, and sine-weaving weavers.
- **The Dreadnought**: a boss with a health bar on waves 5, 10, 15 and 20 — kill it for 500 points and a shower of power-ups.
- **7 power-ups**: Shield, Magnet, Slow-Mo, Double Score, Spread Shot, Frenzy, and the Nova Bomb that clears the whole screen.
- **Combos and streaks**: catch stars inside the window to climb the multiplier (up to x8), chain kills for streak bonuses, clear a wave untouched for a PERFECT WAVE bonus. The combo timer drains visibly and every kill refreshes it.
- **Upgrade shop**: between waves, spend score on Rapid Fire, Extra Bolt, Big Magnet, an Extra Life, or a Shield.
- **Gunships**: enemy ships from wave 4 that fire aimed shots back at you — shoot their bullets down.
- **Black holes**: one per wave from wave 3, warned 2 seconds early — they pull meteors, stars and your ship in, and eat what crosses the horizon for points.
- **Asteroid mining**: shattered boulders burst into three glowing ore shards worth 25 points each.
- **Charge shot**: hold fire to charge a piercing bolt (3 damage, through 3 targets); hold too long and the gun overheats for 2 seconds.
- **Wave themes**: Meteor Shower, Swarm, Gunship Patrol, Calm and Boss waves each change the spawn mix.
- **Juice**: hit-stop on big kills, slow motion on the boss's death, floating score popups, shockwave rings, screen shake, engine trail, and a pulsing combo display.
- **Look**: nebula clouds, a blazing sun, a ringed planet, three parallax star layers, sparkle stars, a vignette, and a low-life danger pulse.
- **Sound**: synthesized WebAudio effects plus a pentatonic music loop that grows an arpeggio layer from wave 5 and turns minor and faster during boss fights.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
